# Visual direction — ScalUp

Reference inspected live at desktop (1440) and mobile (390): Mailman, plus the published ScalUp page and official identity presentation.

## Diagnosis
The existing site repeats centered section headings, visual-above-copy cards, and long uniform graphite bands. The hero portrait and the acquisition model feel separate. Process steps have dead space, the growth system is only labels, and the form has weak step hierarchy.

## Direction
Palette: graphite #1E1C1D, raised graphite #282627, ScalUp green #93FE02, lime #E6FF4B, paper #F5F6F2. Lufga Bold for headings, Regular for supporting copy, Medium for buttons. Content width 1200px; responsive gutters 20–64px; section spacing 72–112px; card padding 24–40px.

Composition: portrait and qualification flow form one hero visual. Paired comparisons precede a broad green solution panel. Services use alternating wide/narrow illustrated cards. Four diagnosis stages are connected by a progress rail. A light metrics and client band separates the dark sections. A snake-shaped acquisition diagram leads into a light form against a dark background. A quiet audience section leads into a large branded closing panel.

Hero: [headline + copy + actions] [portrait + qualification model]
Services: [acquisition — wide] [conversion] / [creative] [data — wide] / [automation — wide] [strategy]
Process: [01] → [02] → [03] → [04]
Acquisition: [campaign → offer → page] ↓ [data ← tracking ← creative] ↓ [automation → funnel → sales]

## Review against the brief
The black/green palette is required by ScalUp, not a generic style choice. Keep the supplied portrait on the right, no eyebrow badges, no invented testimonials, percentages or dashboard statistics. Concept graphics describe actual services. Use clipped text entrances, connector drawing, and process progress rather than applying blur fades to every section. No new runtime dependencies.

Preserve all existing business copy, assets, PT/EN, form fields, validation and submission behavior. Verify all sections at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px, with reduced-motion and keyboard checks.
